Privacy Policy

App: Skarkyvideo post · Last updated 17 October 2026

Who this applies to

Skarkyvideo post is a private, internal tool used only by the owner of the Skärky Video TikTok and YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What the app does

The app uploads short videos that the owner has produced to the owner's own TikTok account inbox as drafts. The owner then reviews and publishes each video manually in the TikTok app.

Information we access

  • Basic TikTok account information (open ID, display name) provided through TikTok Login Kit, used only to confirm which account is connected.
  • The access and refresh tokens TikTok issues, used only to upload videos to that same account.

The app does not read, collect or store any information about other TikTok users, viewers, followers, comments or messages.

Storage and security

Tokens are stored on the owner's private, self-hosted server and are never sold, shared or sent to any third party. Videos are uploaded directly from that server to TikTok.

Sharing

No personal data is shared with, sold to or used for advertising by anyone.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the connection is in use. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services, after which the stored tokens stop working and are deleted.
